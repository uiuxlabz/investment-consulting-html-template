Our Story

From Vision to Industry Leader

Founded in 2005, INVESTA began with a simple mission: to make expert investment guidance accessible to everyone. What started as a small advisory firm in New York has grown into a trusted name in wealth management.

Our founders, veterans of Wall Street, saw a gap in the market for honest, transparent financial consulting. They built INVESTA on the principles of disciplined investing, client education, and long-term relationship building.

Today, we manage over $850 million in assets for more than 1,200 clients worldwide, from individual investors to multinational corporations.
